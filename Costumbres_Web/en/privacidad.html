Privacy & cookie policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

At Costumbres we respect your privacy. This policy explains what personal data we receive, how we use it and how you can exercise your rights, in accordance with Colombian Law 1581 of 2012 and Decree 1074 of 2015 (which compiles Decree 1377 of 2013).

2. What data we receive

This website is informational: it has no forms, user accounts or online payments. We only receive personal data when you choose to message us on WhatsApp or by email, or when you buy at our store:

  • Name.
  • Phone number and/or email address.
  • The content of your messages, including any photos you send.
  • If you buy: delivery address and the details required for electronic invoicing.

We do not request sensitive data or data from minors.

3. How we use your data

  • To answer your questions and quote prices.
  • To confirm availability, prices and reservations.
  • To manage sales, deliveries, exchanges, withdrawals and warranty claims.
  • To issue invoices and meet legal, accounting and tax obligations.
  • To handle requests, complaints and claims.
  • To send you news or promotions, only if you expressly authorize it. You can ask us to stop at any time.

4. Your authorization

When you contact us on your own initiative (via WhatsApp, email or in store), you authorize us to process your data for the purposes above; that is why we show this notice next to our contact channels and in the footer of every page. You can revoke this authorization or ask us to delete your data at any time, unless there is a legal or contractual duty to keep it.

5. Your rights

As the data subject you can:

  • Access, update and correct your personal data.
  • Request proof of the authorization you gave us.
  • Be informed of how we have used your data.
  • Revoke your authorization and/or request deletion of your data.
  • Access your data free of charge.
  • File complaints with Colombia's Superintendencia de Industria y Comercio (SIC).

6. How to exercise your rights

Email us at costumbres.co@hotmail.com with the subject "Personal data", stating your name, your request and how you would like to receive our answer. We answer inquiries within ten (10) business days and claims within fifteen (15) business days, extendable as permitted by law. If you are not satisfied, you can contact the SIC.

7. Third-party services

To communicate with you we use third-party services that process data under their own policies and may store it outside Colombia:

  • WhatsApp (Meta Platforms) for messages.
  • Outlook / Hotmail (Microsoft) for email.

When you visit this site, your browser also connects to Google Fonts (typefaces and icons), the Tailwind CSS CDN (styles) and flagcdn.com (language switcher flags). These services may receive your IP address and technical browser data. We do not sell or share your personal data.

8. Retention and security

We keep your data only as long as necessary for the purposes described and to meet legal obligations (for example, accounting and tax). We take reasonable measures to protect it, such as limiting access to the people who handle your requests.

9. Images of people

Photos of our store that show people are published with their permission. If you appear in one and want it removed, contact us and we will remove it.

10. Cookie policy

This site does not use its own cookies, analytics or advertising.

The Google Maps map does not load automatically: it only appears if you press "Load map", at which point Google may set cookies under its own policy. If you prefer not to load it, use the "Open in Google Maps" link.

If we add analytics or other non-essential cookies in the future, we will ask for your consent before enabling them and update this policy.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The current version will always be published on this page with its update date.
