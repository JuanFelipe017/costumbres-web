Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions and enjoy our handcrafted Colombian artisan pieces.

Are all the handicrafts handmade? expand_more

Yes. Every piece is handcrafted by Colombian artisans using traditional techniques passed down from generation to generation.

How should I care for my handicraft? expand_more

We recommend avoiding moisture, prolonged exposure to sunlight, and contact with chemicals to preserve its beauty and durability.

Can I reserve a handicraft before visiting the store? expand_more

Absolutely. Contact us through WhatsApp and we'll check the availability of the piece you're looking for before your visit.

Do your handicrafts include a warranty? expand_more

Yes. All of our products are covered by a warranty against manufacturing defects. You can view the complete warranty policy by clicking the Warranty button below.
