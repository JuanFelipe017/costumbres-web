Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions and enjoy our handcrafted Colombian artisan pieces.

How can I buy? expand_more

Message us on WhatsApp with the piece you're interested in. Before you pay, we confirm availability, the total price in Colombian pesos, and the payment and delivery options. You can also buy directly at our store.

Do you ship? expand_more

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp or Instagram and we will arrange it. Shipping within Bogotá is free; outside Bogotá there is an additional cost, which we confirm before you pay.

Are all the handicrafts handmade? expand_more

Yes. Every piece is handcrafted by Colombian artisans using traditional techniques passed down from generation to generation.

How should I care for my handicraft? expand_more

We recommend avoiding moisture, prolonged exposure to sunlight, and contact with chemicals to preserve its beauty and durability.

Can I reserve a handicraft before visiting the store? expand_more

Absolutely. Contact us through WhatsApp and we'll check the availability of the piece you're looking for before your visit.

Do your handicrafts include a warranty? expand_more

Yes. Our products have a one (1) year warranty against manufacturing defects, starting on the purchase date. See the conditions in the warranty section below or in our terms and conditions.

Can I exchange or return a piece? expand_more

If you bought remotely (via WhatsApp or email), Colombian law gives you the right to withdraw within five (5) business days after delivery. See how in our terms and conditions.

Warranty

Costumbres Jewelry grants a warranty on its products against manufacturing defects for one (1) year from the date of purchase. This warranty is valid under normal conditions of use and care, and covers the free repair of product defects. If the product cannot be repaired or the defect persists, it will be replaced with an identical item or one with similar characteristics, or the amount paid will be refunded.

This warranty does not cover damage caused by accidents or improper use; broken, scratched or lost stones; chains broken by improper handling; or natural wear and tear due to lack of maintenance. It also does not cover deterioration caused by contact with substances such as chlorine, salt water, perfumes, creams, nail polish or any other chemical or cleaning products.

To make this warranty effective, please contact our customer service by email at costumbres.co@hotmail.com.
