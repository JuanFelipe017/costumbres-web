7. Right of withdrawal

If you bought remotely (via WhatsApp, email or another non-in-person channel), you may withdraw from the purchase within five (5) business days after delivery (Law 1480 of 2011, art. 47). To do so, contact us within that period and return the piece in the same condition you received it. Return shipping costs are paid by you. We refund the money within thirty (30) calendar days, with no deductions.

Withdrawal does not apply, among other cases set by law, to pieces made to your specifications or clearly personalized, or to goods for personal use. For in-store purchases, exchanges are governed by the warranty and by what we agree at the time of purchase.

8. Payment reversal

If you paid for a remote purchase by credit card, debit card or another electronic payment method, you may request a payment reversal in the cases set by law: fraud, an unrequested transaction, a product not received, or a product that does not match your order or is defective (Law 1480 of 2011, art. 51). You must file the complaint with us and notify the payment issuer within five (5) business days of learning of the situation, and return the product if you received it.

9. Warranty

Costumbres Jewelry grants a warranty on its products against manufacturing defects for one (1) year from the date of purchase. This warranty is valid under normal conditions of use and care, and covers the free repair of product defects. If the product cannot be repaired or the defect persists, it will be replaced with an identical item or one with similar characteristics, or the amount paid will be refunded.

This warranty does not cover damage caused by accidents or improper use; broken, scratched or lost stones; chains broken by improper handling; or natural wear and tear due to lack of maintenance.
